App Review Notes — QuizzUp

SIGN-IN
Sign-in is required. Use Continue with Apple, Continue with Google, or Continue with email. Email sign-in supports existing accounts, account creation and password reset. Review access through a working supported provider or a dedicated review account must be verified before submission.

TESTING THE APP
1. Sign in using Continue with Apple, Continue with Google, or Continue with email.
2. Choose a username and tap "Continue".
3. Tap the central yellow Play button, then select Quick duel or Solo. You can also open a topic and choose a mode.
4. During a match, you can tap the ⋯ icon next to an opponent's name to report or block them.
5. After the match, the results screen shows scores and XP earned.

MULTIPLAYER NOTE
Real-time 1v1 requires two connected players. For reliable testing, use Solo mode or coordinate two devices.

NETWORK
The app connects to wss://quizzup-production.up.railway.app/ws for real-time gameplay and HTTPS to the same host for REST endpoints (categories, auth, reports).

LANGUAGES
Change the interface language in Settings. The current question bank is in French; the English option translates the interface, not the quiz questions. The capture fixtures follow the same rule.

PRIVACY
Firebase authentication may provide an email address and name in addition to the user ID. A display name, avatar and game history are used for gameplay, scores and progression. New builds integrate Google AdMob interstitials and UMP. Google test IDs are currently configured; there is no advertising revenue. After four completed games, an available permitted ad may show before starting another game, never on returning Home. If UMP cannot authorise ad requests, gameplay continues without ads. Required privacy options are available in Settings. ATT is requested after a completed game in a safe foreground context, never during a game. Private messages and the in-app friends list are stored for account functionality. There is no device address-book access. Full privacy policy: https://quizzup.io/privacy.html
