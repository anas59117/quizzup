App Review Notes — QuizzUp

SIGN-IN
Sign-in is required. Use Continue with Apple or Continue with Google. The app does not offer an email/password sign-in form. Review access through a working supported provider must be verified before submission.

TESTING THE APP
1. Sign in using Continue with Apple or Continue with Google.
2. Choose a username and tap "Continue".
3. Tap "Quick Play" to find a real-time opponent, or toggle "Solo" to play alone.
4. During a match, you can tap the ⋯ icon next to an opponent's name to report or block them.
5. After the match, the results screen shows scores and XP earned.

MULTIPLAYER NOTE
Real-time 1v1 requires two connected players. For reliable testing, use Solo mode or coordinate two devices.

NETWORK
The app connects to wss://quizzup-production.up.railway.app/ws for real-time gameplay and HTTPS to the same host for REST endpoints (categories, auth, reports).

PRIVACY
Firebase authentication may provide an email address and name in addition to the user ID. A display name, avatar and game history are used for gameplay, scores and progression. No ads or third-party analytics are integrated into the native app. Full privacy policy: https://quizzup-ten.vercel.app/privacy.html
