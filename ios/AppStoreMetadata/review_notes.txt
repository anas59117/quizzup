App Review Notes — QuizzUp

SIGN-IN
Sign-in is required. Use Continue with Apple, Continue with Google, or Continue with email. Email sign-in supports existing accounts, account creation and password reset. Review access through a working supported provider or a dedicated review account must be verified before submission.

TESTING THE APP
1. Sign in using Continue with Apple, Continue with Google, or Continue with email.
2. Choose a username and tap "Continue".
3. Tap the central yellow Play button, then select Quick duel or Solo. You can also open a topic and choose a mode.
4. During a match, you can tap the ⋯ icon next to an opponent's name to report or block them.
5. After the match, the results screen shows scores and XP earned.

MULTIPLAYER NOTE
Real-time 1v1 requires two connected players. For reliable testing, use Solo mode or coordinate two devices.

NETWORK
The app connects to wss://quizzup-production.up.railway.app/ws for real-time gameplay and HTTPS to the same host for REST endpoints (categories, auth, reports).

LANGUAGES
Change the interface language in Settings. The current question bank is in French; the English option translates the interface, not the quiz questions. The capture fixtures follow the same rule.

PRIVACY
Version 1.1 introduces Google AdMob interstitial ads at natural transitions before starting another game: after four distinct completed games, with at least three minutes between displayed ads. Ads never interrupt an active match. Unavailable ads and privacy/network errors do not prevent playing.

Google UMP updates the consent information at every app launch and presents a consent message when required. Settings > Advertising choices (Réglages > Choix publicitaires) opens the applicable privacy choices, or explains when Google does not require a form or the service is temporarily unavailable. European and US state privacy messages are configured in AdMob. After the first completed game, ATT permission is requested in a safe screen only after UMP permits ad requests. Refusing ATT does not prevent playing and uses non-personalized ad requests without IDFA. No ad SDK initialization or ad request occurs while ATT is undetermined. Google publisher first-party IDs and automatic device coarse-location collection are disabled; IP-derived approximate location may still be processed.

Debug and TestFlight builds use Google's test interstitial unit automatically. App Store production builds use the configured QuizzUp unit. To observe a test ad, complete four games, resolve any applicable privacy/ATT prompts, and start a fifth game. Account/app approval by Google and network inventory can affect availability; playing always remains possible.

Firebase authentication may provide an email address and name in addition to the user ID. A display name, avatar and game history are used for gameplay, scores and progression. Private messages, the in-app friends list and community posts/comments are stored for account functionality. Users can choose a photo for a community post through the system photo picker. They can report content and block users. There is no device address-book access. Account deletion is available in Settings. Full privacy policy: https://quizzup.io/privacy.html
