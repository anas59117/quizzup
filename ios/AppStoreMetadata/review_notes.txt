App Review Notes — QuizzUp

DEMO ACCOUNT
A Firebase demo account is available for testing:
- Email: review@quizzup.app
- Password: AppReview2026!

Alternatively, sign in with Apple or Google (both are supported).

TESTING THE APP
1. Sign in using the demo account or your Apple/Google credentials.
2. Choose a username and tap "Continue".
3. Tap "Quick Play" to find a real-time opponent, or toggle "Solo" to play alone.
4. During a match, you can tap the ⋯ icon next to an opponent's name to report or block them.
5. After the match, the results screen shows scores and XP earned.

MULTIPLAYER NOTE
Real-time 1v1 requires two connected players. If no opponent is found within 30 seconds, the match times out. For reliable testing, use Solo mode or coordinate two devices.

NETWORK
The app connects to wss://quizzup-production.up.railway.app/ws for real-time gameplay and HTTPS to the same host for REST endpoints (categories, auth, reports).

PRIVACY
The app collects only the minimum data needed: Firebase Auth UID, display name, and avatar emoji. No tracking, no ads, no third-party analytics. Full privacy policy: https://quizzup-ten.vercel.app/privacy
